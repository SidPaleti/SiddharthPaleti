Honors thesis

Style vs. User: separating how you write from what you write

Human-Centered Robotics Lab · Advised by Dr. Katie Fitzsimons

Two people writing the same letter produce different shapes. How much of that difference is a consistent personal style, and how much comes from the character itself? I represent each handwriting trajectory as a probabilistic movement primitive, find its main directions of variation with kernel PCA, test which of them track the writer and which track the task, then decode a writer's style back into a trajectory.

Robotics research

Centipede: what does it cost a robot to move?

Locomotion in Biology and Robotics Lab · Advised by Dr. Baxi Chong

To compare gaits on the centipede robot, I need its cost of energy: the mechanical work its Dynamixel motors do, divided by weight and distance travelled. I'm building the measurement chain from scratch. A load-cell rig calibrates what a motor actually delivers, and an INA260 sensor reads the current it draws, so torque can be estimated from current alone.
