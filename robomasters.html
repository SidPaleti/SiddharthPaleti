My role

As a dedicated member of the RoboMasters team, I actively contribute to building competition robots designed for combat scenarios. Leveraging my expertise in SolidWorks, I meticulously model and assemble critical components of our robot, ensuring precision and functionality. This design work involves creating detailed parts and assemblies that enhance the robot’s durability and performance. My role extends beyond modeling; I utilize Keil µVision to rigorously test our robot’s components, guaranteeing optimal performance.

In addition to my design responsibilities, I play a significant role in coding the robot’s systems. By programming the motors and other components, I ensure seamless integration and control, which are crucial for the robot’s competitive success. To keep our team aligned and informed, I create and deliver monthly presentations that detail my sub-team’s progress. These presentations are crucial for maintaining transparency and coordinating efforts across the team.

My commitment to RoboMasters doesn’t wane during the summer; I participate in Zoom meetings to update my team on my work in SolidWorks and coding, facilitating seamless manufacturing processes. This hands-on experience in a dynamic, high-stakes environment has not only enhanced my technical skills but also sharpened my ability to communicate complex ideas and lead collaborative projects effectively.
