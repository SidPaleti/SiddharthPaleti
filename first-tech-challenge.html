My role

During my high school years, I had the privilege of participating in the First Tech Challenge (FTC) for five consecutive seasons. FTC is a robotics competition where teams design, build, and program robots to complete a series of tasks, earning points for each successful action. Each match features four teams split into two alliances, requiring seamless cooperation to excel.

As a team captain, I led my team in brainstorming innovative solutions, using both simple materials and advanced CAD software such as SolidWorks and Fusion 360 for prototyping. This experience not only honed my technical skills but also taught me the importance of teamwork and collaboration. I worked closely with my teammates to refine our designs and strategies, ensuring our robot’s compatibility and synergy with our alliance partners.

Throughout the seasons, we faced numerous challenges that required quick thinking and effective problem-solving. I often found myself troubleshooting problems with my teammates, addressing both mechanical and programming issues that arose during competitions. These situations demanded quick fixes to ensure our robot could continue to compete. Late-night CAD sessions were a common occurrence as we worked tirelessly to perfect our designs and implement necessary adjustments.

Over the years, I developed strong leadership and communication skills by delivering presentations on our design process and achievements to judges, resulting in recognition and awards for our team’s efforts. These experiences taught me how to articulate complex technical concepts clearly and confidently.

Our hard work and perseverance paid off, leading us to win the league championship and achieve runner-up at the state championship. This experience solidified my passion for robotics and engineering, paving the way for my continued pursuit of excellence in these fields.
