SolidWorks · Stress analysis

Excalibeer

A fully laser-cut, glue-free bottle opener shaped like Excalibur, with a locking mechanism that holds up to 100 N of finger pressure.

Role
Design and analysis
Tools
SolidWorks, SolidWorks Simulation, laser cutting
Type
Customer design brief
Focus
Locking mechanism without adhesives

Problem

A customer was inspired by the Excalibur sword and wanted to create a bottle opener shaped like it. The design required an innovative locking mechanism, and all parts needed to be laser cut. The challenge was to create a functional, durable mechanism without relying on adhesives.

Method

I began modeling the design in SolidWorks. To create the locking mechanism, I used fixtures that take advantage of a material’s natural ability to return to its original position, essentially functioning as a spring. I incorporated pegs into the stone and slots into the locking mechanism, ensuring precise alignment of all components and eliminating the need for glue. To verify durability, I conducted a stress analysis, applying a 100 N load to represent the extreme case of finger pressure on the locking mechanism.

Solution

The final design allowed the sword-shaped opener to lock securely into the stone and release with a simple press at the top of the mechanism. The interlocking peg-and-slot system ensured stability, and the stress analysis confirmed the mechanism’s ability to withstand significant force. This resulted in a durable, innovative, and fully laser-cut bottle opener inspired by Excalibur.

Design in detail

Every part is laser cut. Alignment comes from the geometry, not from adhesive.

Stress analysis
