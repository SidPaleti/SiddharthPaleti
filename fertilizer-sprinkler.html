Fertilizer Sprinkler

A prototype sprinkler that waters and fertilizes in one pass, with Arduino timing and automatic shut-off, built from cardboard mockup to 3D-printed prototype.

Role
Concept, design, build and code
Tools
SolidWorks, Arduino Uno (C++), 3D printing
Hardware
Servos, barometer, sprinkler junctions
Process
Cardboard model to functional prototype

Problem

Gardening can be highly time-consuming, especially for individuals who cannot dedicate significant time to consistent care. The challenge was to design a system that simplifies gardening by automating watering and fertilizing tasks.

Method

I began with brainstorming and sketching sessions to identify key pain points, which led to the concept of a fertilizer sprinkler that could spray both water and fertilizer simultaneously. Using SolidWorks, I designed the system and integrated an Arduino Uno programmed in C++ to control timing. A barometer was added to enable automatic shut-off, ensuring efficiency and precision. Prototyping started with cardboard models, followed by a functional version built with 3D-printed parts, sprinkler junctions, servos, and a barometer. Along the way, I encountered challenges such as broken parts, delays, and debugging servo control, which required adaptability and iterative problem-solving.

Solution

The final prototype successfully automated the watering and fertilizing process, reducing the time commitment required for gardening. The integration of hardware and software ensured precise control over the sprinkler's operation, while the iterative prototyping process enhanced both my mechanical engineering and coding skills. This project demonstrated my ability to translate a real-world problem into a functional, user-centered solution through research, design, and rapid prototyping.

Design criteria

How the finished prototype measured up.

Primary

Cover up to 4,500 sq ft

Using an H2O-Six Gear Drive Lawn Sprinkler with a telescoping tripod, the system can water and fertilize up to 4,500 square feet, because that sprinkler is rated to cover that area.

Met

Secondary

Fit in a 10 × 18 × 10 in cube

The prototype measures 10 in long, 7 in wide and 8.5 in high. Measuring the build confirmed that it fits the envelope.

Secondary
