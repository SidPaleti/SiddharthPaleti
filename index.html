About me

My work sits where mechanical design meets motion and data. In the Human-Centered Robotics Lab, my Honors thesis asks how much of a person's handwriting is personal style and how much is the character they're writing, and how to separate the two. In the Locomotion in Biology and Robotics Lab, I'm building the measurement chain to find out how much energy a centipede robot's motors really use.

Outside research, I'm on the Penn State RoboMasters team, where I design in SolidWorks, write motor code and present my sub-team's progress. I like projects that go all the way from a sketch to something that works, with the CAD, the circuit, the code and the test rig to prove it.

Honors thesis · Human-Centered Robotics Lab

Style vs. User: separating how you write from what you write

Which part of a handwritten trajectory is a writer's personal style, and which part is just the character? I model, test, and redraw the difference with ProMPs, kernel PCA, ANOVA, and ridge regression.

Centipede: what does it cost a robot to move?

Building the measurement chain for the energy cost of a centipede robot's motors, from a load-cell rig to an INA260 current sensor that lets torque be estimated from current.
